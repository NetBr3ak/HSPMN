## Interpretation

- Variants with NSA's compress + sliding-window branches add O(S^2) compute on top of the 2P forward base.
- ASA mode drops the compress branch → FLOPs/token should be lower than NSA at the same parameter count.
- PC-gate adds a small per-token reduction (norm + sigmoid) - negligible.
- Block-fusion variants (v4-*) have a separate full-attention contextual stream and a separate reflexive stream; total FLOPs are higher than head-fusion at matched params.
